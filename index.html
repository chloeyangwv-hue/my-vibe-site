Mindfulness Journal

My mindfulness journal is a self-designed, self-published journal I created to improve access to wellbeing tools for adolescents. As of today, it's been used by 45 registered clinicians in therapy, Canadian alternative education and Indigenous programs, and homeless youth at Covenant House, a nonprofit where I volunteer. I also host mindfulness workshops at the local library, helping peers understand themselves through self-reflection.

The journal is available in 9 provinces and 37 schools across Canada. I also received multiple grants totalling $3,052 from the Public Health Agency of Canada to expand my journal's reach.

Independent Research

UCLA · 2022–2023

Wrote a 30-page paper uncovering how patterns of behavior driven by technological design, including constant notifications, dopamine-driven rewards, and FOMO created systems of loneliness, anxiety, and chronic sleep deprivation.
